I am an ORISE Fellow at FDA’s Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER), focusing on the validation and regulatory acceptance of <span class="zc-highlight">new approach methodologies</span>.

I earned my PhD at Johns Hopkins University, working with [Dr. Paul Locke](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/faculty/430/paul-a-locke). My research interests center on how scientific evidence informs environmental health policy and public health decisions.
