I am an ORISE Fellow at FDA’s Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER), focusing on the validation and regulatory acceptance of <span class="zc-highlight">new approach methodologies</span>.

I earned my PhD at Johns Hopkins University, working with <a href="https://publichealth.jhu.edu/faculty/430/paul-a-locke" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Paul Locke</a> in the <a href="https://www.jhutoxicologypolicyresearch.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Toxicology Policy Lab</a>. My research interests center on how scientific evidence informs environmental health policy and public health decisions.
