## Ingredients
- Jameson — 3 oz
- Lime Juice — 0.75 oz
- Grapefruit Juice — 1.5 oz
- Honey/Maple Syrup — 0.75 oz
- Optional: Orange Bitter — 2-3 dashes

## Method
Shake → double strain. Ideally, pour over a whole block of frozen Yakult.

## Facts
- ABV ≈ 16%
